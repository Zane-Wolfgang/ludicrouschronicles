“Please, I don’t look good in it…” Rachel had begged, relentlessly tugged, fought, and whimpered, to no avail as her pleas fell on the deaf ears of her friend Lizzie, who simply couldn’t let this preposterous behavior continue. Not dressing adequately to a gathering, where most of the people of Farturn would attend? Blasphemy!

“By the suns, it’s all but a small outfit, see?” Lizzie wasn’t stupid. And while she wasn’t naturally gifted for the eye of fashion (more of pyrotechnics and explosives, though we’ll return to that subject another time), she didn’t have too bad of a taste. Or, what she allowed herself to think, despite how she dressed, which what most deemed her to be a walking catastrophe.

And just maybe to garner some looks, she often found herself accepting gossip as compliments, despite the hurt she received. But by spinning them into jokes… it was easier to look past.

“You tended to appreciate more muted and darker colors… so I thought you’d settle with an ebony gown.” She let a little bit of a shy demeanor filter through as she brought the dress just above her lips, hiding the small smile underneath. “Not too frilly, since I have reason to believe that might catch unwanted wind.”

“No, I can’t, I simply can’t…” Rachel pouted, and not necessarily pathetic, either. It belonged to a woman who believed wholeheartedly she was unable to— “it’s far too much.” She reasoned, brushing her fingers over her arm, curling them around it as a rather depressive gesture.

“I’ll wear my normal outfit, it’s plenty formal; I’m sure the townsfolk will have no issue.”

Lizzie’s expression creased into a frown, and she sulked a little.

“Rachel,” she said gently, tilting her head and lowering the gown she had in her grasp, “darling, you must understand that most individuals have seen you in that get up.” She gestured absently to the discarded belongings on the floor, to which Lizzie had shunned for the time being.

“Continuously wearing it will paint a terrible image. And whilst I’m aware you’re not entirely keen on retaining a standing amongst the public… we can’t have rumors spread.”

“Your mother has poisoned your mind.” Rachel grumbled bitterly, shoulders raised defiantly, yet she took the dress from Lizzie regardless. She may have disagreed, but she was never rude to her friends, no matter how abusive the relationships tend to grow.
