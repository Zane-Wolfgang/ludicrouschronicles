"Mnmghh.." Rachel glowers upon waking up, rising from her bed like a zombie, briefly, before rubbing her eyes.
Now, one would wonder, especially if he were a morning person, *why* of all things she'd be upset upon waking. 
Well, that's generally the worst part of the day, obviously.

However, that was quickly swept under the rug as she clambered off the mattress, Zane shifting afterward. raked his nails against his scalp, drowsy eyes trying to follow the shady figure of Rachel disappearing into her master bathroom.
"Mistress?" His warbled voice was tasked with working so soon during his waking hours, rasped, which initially reminded him to swallow, rubbing his eyes with the heels of his palms.

He leaned against the hallway entrance that led to the doorway of the bathroom, sighing audibly. Always first, wasn't she? Couldn't spare a moment to wait for him, considering he was much more swift when it came to endeavors such as these.
"Arrogant bellend, aren't you, Mistress?" He asks himself endearingly, closing his eyes as he leans his head against the wall.
She was finished and brushed past him with a fleeting kiss to the cheek, which made him jolt a bit. 

While yes, he registered the blue threads closing in on him, he didn't actually anticipate anything with his eyes closed. 
So... he let his guard down to feel a little warm and fuzzy inside.
Just for the moment, though.

Once all was said and done, washed faces, the lovely relief whilst using the throne, clothes on, and morning kisses (somehow, Zane ignored his instinct well enough that it went down... which was a tremendous achievement in his book) following out the door into the living room.
Except, when Rachel opened the door and stepped even a foot out of the sanctuary of her own private space, she shrieked as if she were a banshee, slamming back into Zane, whose threads instantly unraveled out of the back of his neck and uncoiled in front of her, investigating the cause of danger, or just anything—

"Good morning, мой красивый птица~!" 

Zane's threads jerked and receded a little.
His terror was cut short to fill in for fury.
It was the horrendous *clown* ***serial killer*** Mr. Vasily Ragov, bowing in front of them.
He'd dressed lightly, as it was overkill to let himself be enveloped in most of his outfit. Such a small space couldn't fit all his excellence, so he'd make do.
Rachel's terror was dissipating to more of something like 'taken aback', her shoulders scrunched in defense as she righted herself... though a little more confused on how to do it, but... getting there.
Suddenly, two doors burst open, Jackson and Lizzie pouring out, Jackson armed with his knife and an expression leaking out pure *death.*
"WHAT'S GOIN' ON?" He roared, only to set eyes on Vasily, who, had likely come to the same conclusion of why he was only partly dressed in his attire and not bothering to go full throttle, as it appeared to make navigating the household much more difficult.
"Oh by the sun, why is *he* here—" Squeaked Lizzie, ducking behind Jackson as she eyed him judgementally from behind.
Vasily made no move to acknowledge them, however, as he stood ominously beside Rachel, making no apparent move to lay a hand on her.
That didn't mean she wasn't in danger at all, however.

"V... Vasily." She greeted, her brain terribly slow to catch up. Had this been someone out for her blood, she'd most certainly be dead. 
Not being able to react fast enough for the nearest object... in the assassin's trade, it's simply asking for a death sentence.
"I must apologize, Mistress," Zane spoke dully, "for I'm entirely accountable for not sensing a sadistic jester right outside your doorway."

If Mr. Vasily Ragov made any appoint to take offense, he showed nothing of it.
"Darling, if you would so kindly follow me?" Vasily blatantly ignored the existence of Zane, as a matter-of-fact, expression soft despite his mangled face, half-sewn together by thick threads, creases in his smile not even his *own.*

*How could someone* exist *like that?* Zane wondered.
A moment of silence enveloped him as he struggled with a truth bubbling in his skull:
*Then again.. what kind of person does that make* me?
