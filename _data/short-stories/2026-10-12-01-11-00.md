The moment the silence hit, Zane had rooted to the spot.

He’d lay crumpled against a wall, wheezing.

“*Hello?*” He didn’t mean for it to come out so tortured.

But he was so *cold*.

Where was…

The feeling?

That… that feeling of… 

The—*the constant adrenaline.*

His entire body was cold. Not just in his veins, were he was accustomed for it to reside.

“**Hello?**” He asked again, his breathing picking up.

Whatever surge of energy he had previously flickered and extinguished in a heartbeat, causing him to collapse on his knees.

*He was disconnected.*

**Where did his—**

***Where did his powers go…?***
